Featured — currently building

DevLy: an AI OS for students.

DevLy pulls everything a student needs — mentorship, workspace, courses, and planning — into one AI-native system, instead of five disconnected apps.

01

AI Mentor

A always-available guide that answers questions in context of what the student is actually working on.

02

AI Workspace

Where the student's notes, tasks, and material live alongside the assistant that helps make sense of them.

03

Course Generator

Turns a topic or goal into a structured course, instead of a student having to assemble one from scattered sources.

04

Personalized Learning

Adapts pacing and material to how a specific student learns, not a one-size curriculum.

05

AI Planner

Keeps deadlines, study time, and goals organized so students spend less time managing and more time learning.
